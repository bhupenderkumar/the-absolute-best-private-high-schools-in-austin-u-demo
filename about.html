Our Mission & History

The Absolute Best Private High School in Austin was founded in 2005 by a group of dedicated educators who shared a vision of creating a unique learning environment that would provide students with the skills, knowledge, and values necessary to succeed in an ever-changing world.

Our teaching approach is centered around project-based learning, where students work collaboratively to solve real-world problems and develop critical thinking, creativity, and communication skills.

From debate team to soccer, our extracurricular programs are designed to help students develop their passions, build lasting relationships, and cultivate a sense of responsibility and community service.
